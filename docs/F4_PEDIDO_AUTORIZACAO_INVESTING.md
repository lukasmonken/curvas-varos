# Pedido de autorização ao Investing.com (rascunho para a equipe enviar) — Q16

Contexto: `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, Q16 e Q17; `docs/F3_DIAGNOSTICO_CDS.md`. Os termos do Investing.com (§10(a), §10(c), §14) proíbem uso comercial, extração automatizada e exibição dos dados em site sem permissão escrita. O próprio site indica tools@investing.com para pedidos de dados. Antes de enviar, ajuste remetente, assinatura e o uso pretendido, e confirme com o jurídico o que pedir (só uso interno, ou também exibição).

---

**To:** tools@investing.com
**Subject:** Permission request – automated daily retrieval of Brazil CDS (USD) data for internal use

Hello,

We are VAROS, an investment research company in Brazil. Our team currently consults your pages for Brazil sovereign CDS in USD every business day, for these tenors:

6M, 1Y, 2Y, 3Y, 4Y, 5Y, 7Y, 10Y and 20Y
(e.g. https://br.investing.com/rates-bonds/brazil-cds-5-years-usd-historical-data)

We would like to ask for written permission to:

1. retrieve the end-of-day values of these 9 series automatically, once per business day, from a scheduled job (low volume: about 9 requests per day, with an identifiable User-Agent and rate limiting); and
2. use the values internally in our valuation assumptions.

If possible, please also let us know:

- whether displaying these values (or annual figures derived from them) on our website would require a separate license, and on what terms;
- whether there is an official data feed, export or partner product for this, and its pricing;
- the original data provider for these series and any attribution you require.

We will not attempt to bypass any access control. Until we hear from you, our team will keep consulting the pages manually.

Thank you,
